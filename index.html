Who We Are

Bridging the Gap Between Medicine & the Law

Veridicus Medical Forensic Consultancy was founded by board-certified physicians and forensic specialists committed to delivering objective, evidence-based expert opinion in complex healthcare litigation.

We work exclusively with legal professionals — plaintiff and defense attorneys, insurance carriers, and regulatory bodies — providing the clinical clarity needed to build, challenge, or settle cases involving healthcare crimes and professional misconduct.

Our consultants have testified in state and federal courts across the country, reviewed thousands of medical records, and contributed to landmark healthcare fraud prosecutions.

How It Works

Our Engagement Process

I

Initial Consultation

A confidential call with our intake team to assess case type, jurisdiction, timeline, and expert specialty requirements. No charge for initial screening.

II

Expert Matching

We identify the optimal specialist from our network — matching by clinical subspecialty, geographic proximity to jurisdiction, and litigation experience.

III

Records Review

Our expert conducts a thorough, systematic review of all relevant medical documentation, imaging studies, and records under strict confidentiality protocols.

IV

Deliverable & Support

A comprehensive written opinion is delivered in court-ready format. Our consultants remain available for deposition preparation, rebuttal, and trial support.
